# Private Travel Skill Research
A private travel skill is being developed to search flights and stays, track chosen routes and dates, and send phone notifications when prices drop. The search will run on the user's PC to maintain privacy. This report summarizes the findings on free or cheap ways to get flight and hotel prices, existing price-alert tools, and Israeli specifics.

## Flight and Hotel Price APIs
Several APIs offer free tiers for accessing flight and hotel prices, including Amadeus Self-Service, Duffel, Kiwi Tequila, Skyscanner partners, Google Flights via SerpAPI-like, Booking/Expedia affiliate APIs, and Airbnb [1]. However, the limits and terms of these APIs vary, and caching, polling frequency, and scraping rules must be considered [2]. For example, Google Flights offers a price history graph and low-price calendar, but its price-tracking alert feature is reactive, not predictive [1].

## Existing Price-Alert Tools
Existing price-alert tools, such as Google Flights, Hopper, Kayak, and Skyscanner, have different polling frequencies and functionalities [3]. Google Flights' price-tracking alert feature notifies users of significant changes, while Kayak sends daily digests or real-time alerts on roughly 10% moves [4]. Hopper is a phone app built around predictions and push notifications, and Skyscanner ties alerts to saved searches [5]. The frequency of polling varies among these tools, with some checking prices daily and others in real-time [6].

## Israeli Specifics
For Israeli users, El Al, Israir, and Arkia are relevant airlines to consider [7]. The Israeli Shekel (ILS) is the local currency, and a reliable currency conversion source is necessary [8]. Ben Gurion Airport is a major hub, and routes from this airport should be included in the search [9]. A safe design for the travel skill should include a polling budget, de-duplication, alert thresholds, and a browser-assisted fallback [10].

## Language and Weight
The language used for the travel skill should be English, with the option to add Hebrew support in the future. The weight of the application should be minimal to ensure fast performance on the user's PC.

## Conclusion
The research findings indicate that a private travel skill can be developed using free or cheap APIs and existing price-alert tools as references. Israeli specifics, such as El Al and Ben Gurion Airport, should be considered, and a safe design should include a polling budget and browser-assisted fallback.
