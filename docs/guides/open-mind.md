# Open mind

**עברית בקצרה:** "פתח את הראש" (Open mind): מקליטים דיבור חופשי, המערכת מתמללת, ממיינת לרעיונות, משימות ושאלות בדבריך שלך, ואפשר לחקור כל רעיון בשיחת מחקר או לשמור כהערה בידע. הדף ב-`#/ideas`.

## What it does
A brain dump becomes sorted items (ideas, tasks, questions) kept in your own words, which you review and send on: start a research chat, save a note where Knowledge reads it, or turn it into a task.

## Prerequisites
A chat model and a speech-to-text model ([voice-mode.md](voice-mode.md)); web access if you want research chats.

## Steps
1. Open **Open mind** from the sidebar.
2. Record and talk; stop when done. The recording is transcribed.
3. Review the sorted items: edit, merge, split or drop any.
4. Pick one of the ways on: research it (a web-enabled chat), save as a note, or make a task. Past chats related to an idea are suggested.

## Verify
Say two ideas and a question; three items appear and one starts a research chat.

## Troubleshooting
- Empty result: the transcript was too short or the model is too small.
- Hebrew mixed with English: fine; the model keeps your wording.

## Safety
Incognito chats are left out of the search for related chats. Recordings are transcribed on your computer unless you chose a cloud speech service.
