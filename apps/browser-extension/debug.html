Let KesherAI debug pages?

A task in KesherAI wants its exact browser tools here: read the page's structure, see the console and the network, press keys and take pictures without bringing the tab to the front.

While it works, Chrome shows a bar: "KesherAI started debugging this browser". That is expected. Pressing Cancel on that bar stops KesherAI right away.

  • Only for tasks you ask for, only on sites you allowed, and the app still asks before acting.
  • Its tabs are kept in a "KesherAI" tab group. It lets go after 5 idle minutes.
  • You can take this back in chrome://extensions → KesherAI → Details.
