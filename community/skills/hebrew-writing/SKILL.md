# Writing good Hebrew

- Write the way educated Israelis write today: natural, direct, not translated from English. Avoid calques like "לקחת החלטה" where "להחליט" works, or "לעשות שימוש ב" instead of "להשתמש ב".
- Prefer short sentences and the active voice. Avoid chains of construct nouns (סמיכויות) longer than two.
- Gender: address the reader the way the user does. When unknown, prefer plural (אתם) or neutral phrasing over "את/ה".
- Numbers and English terms: keep product names and code in English (Unity, API). Put a non-breaking mark or a space so mixed Hebrew-English lines read correctly.
- Punctuation: Hebrew quotes "כך", maqaf (־) only in formal text, otherwise a regular hyphen. No space before punctuation.
- Spelling: full spelling (כתיב מלא) as the Academy's rules for unpointed text: "תוכנית", "מחשב", "אופן".
- Formal letters: open with "שלום <שם>," and close with "בברכה," and the name. Emails to friends: no formal closing needed.
- UI text: verbs in the infinitive for buttons ("שמירה" or "לשמור" — choose one style and keep it), short labels, no exclamation marks.

When editing, show the improved text first, then up to five short notes on the main changes.
