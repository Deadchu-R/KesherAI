# Meeting notes

1. If you were given an audio or video file, transcribe it first with `transcribe_audio` (with `timestamps: true` for long meetings).
2. Read the whole transcript before writing.
3. Write, in the language of the meeting:

**Summary** — 3 to 6 sentences: what the meeting was about and where it ended.

**Decisions** — a list; each decision in one line, with who decided when it is clear.

**Action items** — a list of `Owner — task — due date` (write "no date" when none was said). Only tasks someone agreed to do.

**Open questions** — what was left unresolved.

Rules:
- Do not invent names, dates or numbers. If a speaker is unclear, write "someone".
- Keep quotes short and only when the exact words matter.
- With timestamps, add the time of each decision, like (12:40).
