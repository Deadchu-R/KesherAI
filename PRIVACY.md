# KesherAI privacy policy

KesherAI (the desktop app and its browser extension) collects no data and sends nothing to its makers.

## The browser extension

- **What it reads:** the tabs and pages you ask KesherAI to work with (their address, title, text and a screenshot when
  a task needs one), and the page you ask about in the side panel.
- **Where it goes:** only to the KesherAI app on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`), through a connection that needs the
  key you paste from KesherAI's settings. Nothing goes to any other server, and web pages cannot connect to it.
- **What it keeps:** the connection key and port, in the browser's local storage. Nothing else.
- **What it never does:** type into password or payment-card fields, sell or share data, or use it for ads or
  credit decisions. Every action can be set to ask you first in KesherAI.

## The app

The app runs on your computer. Chats, files and models stay there. It talks to an online service only when you
connect one yourself (for example a cloud model provider, Google Drive or Telegram), and then only to do what you
asked.
