## Summary
# Private Travel Skill Research
This research aims to create a private travel skill for searching flights and stays, tracking chosen routes and dates, and sending phone notifications when prices drop. The search will run on the user's PC, ensuring privacy, and not on a cloud service. The findings will focus on free or cheap ways to get flight and hotel prices, existing price-alert tools, and Israeli specifics for flight routes and currency conversion.

## Free and Cheap APIs for Flight and Hotel Prices
Several APIs offer free tiers or cheap options for accessing flight and hotel prices. According to [1], some of the best flight APIs with free tiers include Thunderbit, Amadeus, Duffel, Kiwi.com Tequila, and Skyscanner via RapidAPI. Hermeseus [2] offers a free travel API for flights, hotels, and activities, with a 1% per issued ticket fee. However, the free tiers and pricing models vary, and some APIs have limitations on caching, polling frequency, and scraping rules.

## Existing Price-Alert Tools and Polling Frequencies
Existing price-alert tools, such as CamelCamelCamel, Keepa, and Honey, offer free or low-cost options for tracking prices [9]. These tools typically use browser extensions
