## Goal
Deep feature research on KesherAI's MAIN CHAT, Roee's top priority: it must feel like the Claude app — efficient, fast, fluent and clear, with full Hebrew (RTL) and English support and every tool working smoothly. Compare end to end with Claude (web/desktop/mobile: projects, artifacts, tools, research, memory, approvals, voice), ChatGPT, Gemini, LobeHub, Open WebUI, Msty, LM Studio, Jan, Cursor chat. Cover: composer and attachments, streaming and progress feedback, tool calls shown to the user, approvals, artifacts/canvas/docs, model picking and auto routing, memory and projects, search and history, long chats (context, compaction), errors and retries, RTL/bidi text and mixed Hebrew-English, speed tricks (prefill, caching, speculative decoding for local models), mobile parity, accessibility.
Read first (this repo, read only): docs/WORK_LIST.md (what exists and the backlog), docs/FEATURE_REVIEW.md, docs/VISION.md, and the relevant pages under apps/web/src/pages to know what KesherAI already has. Then research the competitors on the web (primary sources from the last 6 months: official docs, changelogs, repos, reviews).
