## Goal
Before KesherAI redesigns its interface (desktop and phone): how do leading AI apps structure navigation and visual design today — ChatGPT desktop, Claude desktop, Gemini, LM Studio, LobeHub, Open WebUI, Msty, Cursor, Raycast AI? Cover: one unified menu vs separate sections, colour-coding of areas, light and dark themes, how projects/chats/tools/settings are grouped, how the phone version differs, and RTL (Hebrew) support. End with a recommended structure for an app that has chat, editors (image, video, sound, 3D), game-engine tools, models and agents.

## Report
Headings: Patterns (with which app does what), Navigation, Colour and themes, Phone, RTL, Recommendation. A numbered Sources list with links. At most 1000 words.
