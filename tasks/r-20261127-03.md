## Goal
Research only (no code). KEEP IT UNDER 1,200 WORDS.
KesherAI (Electron + Node, runs on the user's PC) wants a travel tool like Skyscanner but private: searches run locally, each in a fresh isolated browser profile with no cookies and no account, so sites cannot learn who searches. Find end to end: how Skyscanner, Google Flights, Kayak, Kiwi and Hopper search and present results (filters, flexible dates, price calendar, "anywhere", map, sort by best/cheapest/fastest); which sources are allowed and free or have a free tier (Amadeus Self-Service, Kiwi Tequila, Duffel, the open-source fast-flights library, SerpAPI, Booking/Hostelworld/Airbnb affiliate or partner APIs) and their terms on automated access; whether reading the sites as a guest in a fresh browser is allowed by their terms; whether cookie-based price raising is real (evidence); how price-watch alerts are usually built (polling interval, price history). Anything paid must be an optional key the user enters. Include a "Language and weight" part for every platform (Windows/macOS/Linux, phone showing results from the PC), and end with a "## Tasks" section of 5-8 concrete tasks (S/M/L).

## Acceptance
Competitor table, allowed sources with terms, privacy evidence, watch design, Language and weight, Tasks.
