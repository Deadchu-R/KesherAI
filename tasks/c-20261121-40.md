## Goal
Roee thinks KesherAI's Downloads page (models, engines, tools: big multi-GB files) can learn a lot from the downloads page of Epic Games' new launcher, now in preview. Research the new Epic Games Launcher preview end to end (official Epic announcements/blog, changelogs, hands-on reviews and videos from the last months), focusing on its downloads/library experience: queue and order, pause/resume, per-item and total progress, speed and time left, bandwidth limits, install location and disk-space checks, verify/repair, updates, background and "install when idle", notifications, how errors are shown. Compare briefly with Steam's downloads page and the Xbox app. Then list what KesherAI's Downloads page should copy (KesherAI downloads Hugging Face models of 1–20 GB, Unreal/Unity add-ons and tools, on a home PC, also controlled from a phone).

## Report
English, about 1000–1500 words: 1) What the new Epic launcher's downloads page does (with what is new vs the old launcher), 2) Steam/Xbox in one short table, 3) Ten concrete improvements for KesherAI's Downloads page ranked by value, each one line with size S/M/L, 4) Numbered sources with links. If something could not be confirmed, say so.
